PHASE 3 · VERSION 3.2.0

Your complete programme

Four strength days, one bodyweight reset day, spin, and optional easy movement. Full lifting sessions allow around 40–55 minutes including unilateral work, warm-up and recovery. For a shorter visit, use Express: the first three movements, approximately 25–35 minutes.

No hip-bar loading, landmine work, travelling carries or sled work. Alternatives replace an exercise; they do not add extra sets. These are general fitness choices, not a rehabilitation plan.

Effort and progression

Weeks 1–2: RIR 3. Weeks 3–4: RIR 2–3. Week 5: reduce 3-set exercises to 1 set and 2-set exercises to 1 set, at RIR 4–5. Weeks 6–7: RIR 2. Week 8: RIR 2–3. Loaded hinges remain RIR 3 outside the deload. Reset and optional sessions remain easy. Timed holds stop before posture fails; spin uses a comfortable, recoverable effort.

RIR is the number of clean repetitions you could still do. At the top of the prescribed range across all sets, with comfortable technique and recovery, consider the smallest load increase next time. Do not chase a load increase through pain.

Rest at least as long as shown, and longer when needed. On unilateral work, complete both sides before the main rest, with extra rest between sides when necessary. The reset and spin days do not need gym-style lifting progression.

Eight-week review

The app calculates the block week and flags the review after 56 days. It does not automatically generate a new exercise programme. Export your history for a personalised review. Starting another block repeats this programme while retaining history.

Symptoms

Stop exercises that cause hamstring pain. Recurring or worsening symptoms merit individual assessment by a physiotherapist. Use a comfortable range rather than forcing a stretch.
